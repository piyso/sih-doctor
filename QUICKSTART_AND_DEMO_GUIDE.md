## 🎬 The 3-Minute Grand Jury Demonstration Flow

To demonstrate the system to judges or evaluators in an impressive, zero-mock live flow:

### Step 1: Patient Self-Intake at the Kiosk (<kbd>1</kbd>)
1. Open the **Patient MediKiosk** (`http://localhost:5173/?mode=kiosk`).
2. Select any language (e.g. English, Hindi, Punjabi, Bengali, Tamil, etc.).
3. Type a patient name (e.g. *Rajesh Sharma*, Age 45, Male).
4. Tap the 3D Body Mannequin on the **Chest** or **Stomach**.
5. Select symptoms (e.g. *Severe retrosternal chest tightness radiating to left shoulder, sweating*).
6. Complete the brief 3-question Ayush Prakriti screen and click **Generate OPD Token**.
7. The system instantly renders a statutory **Thermal OPD Token & Verhoeff $D_5$ ABHA QR Slip**.

1. Switch to the **Doctor Clinical Cockpit** (`http://localhost:5173/?mode=doctor`).
2. Notice *Rajesh Sharma* is **already in the live queue** with ESI Tier 1 Red Flag alert!
3. Click on the patient to open the pre-populated case sheet.
4. Click **Start Ambient Recording** to capture physical microphone speech, or type live clinical notes.
5. In the Prescriber, prescribe **Warfarin** (Allopathy) and then add **Yogaraja Guggulu** (Ayurveda).
6. **Watch the Real-Time Truth Engine Interlock fire instantly!** A high-contrast alert displays: *Severe Hemorrhage Risk: Coumarin synergism with Guggulsterones.*

### Step 3: Pharmacy Dispensing & Barcode Gate (<kbd>3</kbd>)
